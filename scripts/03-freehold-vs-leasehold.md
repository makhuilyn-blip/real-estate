# Script 03 — Freehold vs Leasehold: Does It Actually Matter?

**Format:** Vertical short
**Length:** ~40–50 sec
**Goal:** Clarify the difference and why leasehold isn't automatically "bad."

---

## HOOK (0:00–0:03)
**Visual:** Countdown timer overlaid on a condo building.
**VO / On-screen text:**
> "Your home has an expiry date... unless it's freehold. Here's what that actually means."

## SETUP (0:03–0:10)
**VO:**
> "In Singapore, most private property is either freehold or leasehold — usually 99 years. And no, leasehold isn't automatically the 'bad' option."

## POINT 1 — The basics (0:10–22)
**Visual:** Two icons — infinity symbol (freehold) vs countdown (99-year leasehold).
**VO:**
> "Freehold means you own the land indefinitely. Leasehold means you own it for a fixed period — after that, it returns to the state, unless it's part of a government renewal scheme."
**On-screen text:** "Freehold = forever. Leasehold = fixed term."

## POINT 2 — Why leasehold isn't automatically bad (0:22–35)
**Visual:** Price tag comparison, then CPF usage graphic.
**VO:**
> "Leasehold properties are usually cheaper, and most HDB flats — where the majority of Singaporeans live — are 99-year leasehold anyway. The key thing to watch is lease decay, especially once a flat crosses 60 years remaining, since that can affect your CPF usage and bank loan eligibility."
**On-screen text:** "Watch the remaining lease, not just the label."

## POINT 3 — What to actually check (0:35–45)
**VO:**
> "Before you buy, don't just ask 'freehold or leasehold' — ask how many years are left, and how that affects your ability to use CPF and get a loan."

## CTA / OUTRO (0:45–50)
**VO:**
> "Understanding lease decay could save you from a bad decision decades down the road. Follow for more Singapore property basics."
**On-screen text:** "Follow — Singapore Property 101"
