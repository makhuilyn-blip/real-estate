# Script 01 — BTO vs Resale: Which Should You Buy First?

**Format:** Vertical short (TikTok / IG Reels / YT Shorts)
**Length:** ~45–55 sec
**Goal:** Educate first-time buyers on the core tradeoff between BTO and resale HDB flats.

---

## HOOK (0:00–0:03)
**Visual:** Split screen — construction crane (BTO) vs move-in-ready flat (resale).
**VO / On-screen text:**
> "Should you buy a BTO or a resale flat? One saves you money. One saves you YEARS."

## SETUP (0:03–0:10)
**Visual:** Presenter to camera, casual tone.
**VO:**
> "If you're a first-time buyer in Singapore, this is probably the biggest decision you'll make before you even own a home. Let's break it down in 40 seconds."

## POINT 1 — Price (0:10–20)
**Visual:** Price comparison graphic ($ sign, bar chart style).
**VO:**
> "BTO flats are built by HDB and sold at subsidised prices — way below market value. Resale flats are sold by existing owners at market rate, which is usually higher, but you can also get grants like the CPF Housing Grant depending on your eligibility."
**On-screen text:** "BTO = cheaper, but you're buying 'off-plan'"

## POINT 2 — Waiting Time (0:20–30)
**Visual:** Calendar flipping pages / clock spinning.
**VO:**
> "The catch with BTO? Construction time. You could wait 3 to 5 years before you get your keys. Resale flats are move-in ready — sometimes within a few months of signing."
**On-screen text:** "BTO = wait years. Resale = move in months."

## POINT 3 — Location & Lease (0:30–40)
**Visual:** Map pins on Singapore map, then lease countdown graphic.
**VO:**
> "BTO locations are limited to new launches in select towns. With resale, you can buy almost anywhere in Singapore — but check the remaining lease, because that affects your CPF usage and future resale value."
**On-screen text:** "Resale = more locations. Watch the lease decay."

## CTA / OUTRO (0:40–50)
**Visual:** Presenter back to camera, friendly close.
**VO:**
> "So — tight budget and can wait? BTO. Need a home now and want flexibility on location? Resale. Follow for more Singapore property basics explained in under a minute."
**On-screen text:** "Follow for more 🇸🇬 property tips"

---

### Production notes
- Keep captions burned in — most viewers watch muted.
- Use a consistent lower-third "Singapore Property 101" bug for series branding.
- Pin a comment with a 1-line disclaimer: "Not financial advice — always check current HDB eligibility rules."
