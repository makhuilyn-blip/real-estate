# Script 02 — What is ABSD? (Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty)

**Format:** Vertical short
**Length:** ~40–50 sec
**Goal:** Explain ABSD simply so buyers understand why it changes their numbers.

---

## HOOK (0:00–0:03)
**Visual:** Bold text slam-in over a stack of cash/receipt.
**VO / On-screen text:**
> "Buying a second property in Singapore? The government wants a cut BEFORE you even move in."

## SETUP (0:03–0:12)
**VO:**
> "It's called ABSD — Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty. It's an extra tax on top of the normal stamp duty, and how much you pay depends on who you are and how many properties you already own."

## POINT 1 — Who pays what (0:12–28)
**Visual:** Simple tiered graphic — Citizens / PRs / Foreigners, 1st / 2nd / 3rd property.
**VO:**
> "Singapore Citizens buying their first home pay zero ABSD. Buy a second property, and ABSD kicks in. Permanent Residents pay ABSD from their very first purchase. Foreigners pay the highest rate, even on their first property."
**On-screen text:** "Rates change — always check the latest IRAS table."

## POINT 2 — Why it exists (0:28–38)
**Visual:** Housing market graph cooling down.
**VO:**
> "ABSD is a cooling measure — it's designed to slow down investment demand so homes stay accessible for people who actually need to live in them."

## CTA / OUTRO (0:38–48)
**VO:**
> "If you're planning to buy a second property, factor ABSD into your budget from day one — it can be a huge chunk of change. Follow for more Singapore property terms explained simply."
**On-screen text:** "Follow — Singapore Property 101"

---

### Production notes
- ABSD rates are politically sensitive and change with cooling measure updates — do NOT hardcode percentages in the video; direct viewers to check IRAS.gov.sg for current rates.
